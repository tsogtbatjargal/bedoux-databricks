# Write a Sentinel story

Paths below are relative to the repository root.

Read `docs/sentinel/writing.md`, the chapter in `docs/sentinel/roadmap.md`, and
the available chapter evidence. For a kickoff or proposal, use future tense and
identify planned behavior. For a results post, tie every outcome and number to
an actual recorded run. Ask for a missing personal observation when it matters;
otherwise omit it instead of inventing the author's feelings or experience.

Make one point: what went wrong, the decision it prompted, what was built, what
the evidence shows, and what remains limited. Use the ancient-strategy connection
briefly, as an interpretation. Verify direct quotations and name the translation;
otherwise paraphrase without quotation marks.

Sound like a developer explaining a concrete piece of work to a colleague.
Use ordinary sentences, varied rhythm, and specific details. Remove stock
openings, exaggerated claims, repeated dramatic contrasts, and engagement bait.
Do not manufacture typos or hide material agent assistance to simulate authorship.

Include a stable chapter reference when available. If the branch/tag is still
local or the evidence is missing, leave the draft explicitly marked unpublished;
do not invent a working public link. Suggest one visual from actual evidence.
For a demo script, include what appears on screen and where a replay is used.
Return or save a draft within the user's scope. Never post it automatically.
